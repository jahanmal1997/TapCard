Frequently asked questions

How do I add my card to Apple Wallet?

Fill in at least your first and last name, then tap Add to Wallet on the home screen and confirm in the Wallet sheet.

What does my QR code contain?

A standard contact card (vCard) with the details you entered. Anyone can scan it with their phone's camera, no app needed, and save you as a contact.

How do I scan someone else's card?

Open the Scan tab and point the camera at their QR code. You can then save them to your contacts.

I changed my details. How do I update my Wallet card?

Edit your profile in the app and tap Add to Wallet again. Apple Wallet will replace the old card.

How do I delete my data?

All your details are stored on your device. Go to Settings → Reset Profile Data, or delete the app. To remove a card from Apple Wallet, open it in Wallet and tap Remove Pass.
