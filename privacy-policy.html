TapCard Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

TapCard ("the app") is a digital business card app published by nxgits ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how. In short: we do not have user accounts, we do not sell your data, we do not track you, and we do not store your information on our servers.

1. Information you enter

To build your card you may enter your name, job title, company, phone number, email address, a short bio, social media links, a profile photo and a company logo. This information is saved only on your device. You can change or delete it at any time, and "Reset Profile Data" in Settings erases it.

2. Adding your card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet

When you tap "Add to Wallet", the app sends your card details (the fields above, your photo/logo and your chosen card colors) over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our pass-generation server. The server uses them only to create the signed wallet pass and returns it immediately. The server does not save your card details. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical request logs (such as IP address and time of request) for a short period for security and reliability.

The pass is then stored by Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. If you use Google Wallet, your card details are sent to Google to create the pass. Their handling is governed by Apple's and Google's privacy policies.

3. Camera

The camera is used only to scan QR codes on other people's TapCards. Scanning happens on your device. We do not record, store or upload images or video.

4. Contacts

With your permission, the app saves a scanned card to your phone's Contacts. We do not read, upload or share your address book.

5. Photos

With your permission, the app lets you pick a profile photo or company logo from your photo library. Only the photo you select is used, and it stays on your device unless you add your card to a wallet (see section 2).

6. Sharing your card

Your card is shared only when you choose to share it, for example by showing your QR code or sending a contact file. Anyone who scans your QR code receives the contact details on your card.

7. What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.

8. Children

TapCard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email info@nxgits.com.
